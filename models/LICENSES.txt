3D gun models used by this game (models/*.glb)
==============================================

All files below are unmodified GLB files by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com),
released under CC0 1.0 Universal / Public Domain Dedication
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is not required;
credit is given in the lobby footer as a courtesy. The game renames the files and,
at runtime only, converts their materials to Lambert, tints a few variants and adds
small primitive add-ons (suppressor, LMG box mag + bipod). None of these models are
derived from VALORANT / Riot Games designs; they are generic real-world style guns.
