### Guiding Light update: steering + explicit detonation
Reference (https://wiki.playvalorant.com/en-us/Guiding_Light): HOLD FIRE steers direction and altitude toward the crosshair. Implemented: client sends `hg:1` (and fire:0) in the input message while LMB is held with a hawk in flight; server steerHawk turns hd toward the owner's aim at GUIDING.turn rad/s, and ignores gun fire while hg.
